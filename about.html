Official partner accreditations

Partner status matters because it signals platform expertise, ongoing investment in training, and direct access to the technology partners that drive Shopify performance. Below are the formal accreditations Rainy City holds, plus the tier and renewal date for each.

Shopify Plus Partner

Shopify's most senior partnership tier. Available only to agencies with a proven track record on Shopify Plus, demonstrated technical capability, and active client portfolio. Reviewed and renewed annually by Shopify.

Google Partner

Google's partnership programme for agencies running paid media and ecommerce technical integrations. Required for advanced Google Shopping integration support and Google Ads campaigns for clients.

Meta Business Partner

Meta's formal partnership programme for agencies working on Facebook and Instagram commerce integrations. Required for advanced catalogue management, conversions API setup, and Meta-specific commerce work.

What it means to be a Shopify Platinum Partner

Anyone can build on Shopify. Not every agency that calls itself "a Shopify agency" holds formal partner status. The distinction matters because Plus Partners get direct access to Shopify's technical team, early access to platform features, and joint accountability for client outcomes. It changes what an agency can actually deliver.

Direct access to Shopify's technical team

Plus Partners have a named contact at Shopify. When complex technical issues hit, we escalate directly to Shopify's engineering team rather than waiting in a queue.

Early access to platform features

New Shopify capabilities - Checkout Extensibility, Functions, B2B features - reach Plus Partners months before general availability. That means we can plan client roadmaps around what's coming, not just what's here.

Joint accountability for client outcomes

Plus Partner status is performance-reviewed annually. Shopify checks our active accounts, client retention, and delivery quality. We hold the status because we've earned it on real client work, not because we ticked an application form.

How Rainy City got here

Rebecca Worsley founded Rainy City in 2017. The agency was Manchester-based from day one, Shopify-focused from day one, and built around one premise: scaling DTC brands don't fit neatly into agency templates. The work has been Shopify-only ever since — through Shopify Plus launches, through the rise of headless and back to native, through eight years of platform changes. The discipline has been the same throughout.

Today, Rainy City is a team of designers, developers, and strategists working with ambitious DTC brands across the UK, Europe, and the US. We focus on three things — Shopify design, Shopify development, and conversion rate optimisation — and we do them at a higher level than agencies twice our size.
