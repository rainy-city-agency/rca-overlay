Questions buyers ask before they sign off

Honest answers to the questions we get on every migration discovery call.

How long does a Shopify migration take?

Most migrations run between 6 and 12 weeks, depending on the complexity of your data, the number of integrations involved, and the design work required. We give every project a fixed timeline after Step 1 (Discovery and Scoping). Once locked in, we work to that timeline and tell you immediately if anything is at risk.

Will we lose our SEO rankings during the migration?

Not when it is done properly. SEO preservation is Step 3 of our process and one of the most common reasons brands choose a specialist agency over a generalist build team. We map every existing URL to a new equivalent, update metadata, port across schema markup, and submit the new sitemap to Google. The aim is to launch without any drop in organic traffic. In practice, most clients see improvements within a few months.

What about our existing customer data and order history?

Everything migrates across. Customer accounts, addresses, order history, subscriptions, loyalty points, and any custom metadata you have built up over the years. We validate every record before launch and reconcile the totals against your source platform. If a customer logs in after launch, they see their full purchase history.

Do we have to be on Shopify Plus, or will standard Shopify work?

Depends on your trading volume, your customisation needs, and your team structure. Standard Shopify works for many brands. Shopify Plus becomes essential at higher transaction volumes, when you need advanced checkout customisation, or when you are running multiple storefronts. Step 1 of our process includes a recommendation on which is right for you.

What does a Shopify migration cost?

Migration projects vary significantly based on data complexity, design scope, and integrations required. Smaller migrations typically start in the low five figures. Larger Shopify Plus migrations with custom development can run into six figures. We provide a fixed cost after Step 1, not an hourly estimate. No surprise invoices, no scope-creep billing.
