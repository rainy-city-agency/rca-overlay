Shopify vs Magento

A practical, agency-side comparison of the two leading ecommerce platforms for scaling brands. Where each fits, where each fails, and what most brands miss in the decision.

The quick answer

For most scaling DTC brands in the £1M-£50M revenue range, Shopify and Shopify Plus deliver better total cost of ownership, faster time to launch, and a simpler operational model than Magento.

Magento (now Adobe Commerce) still makes sense for very specific scenarios: complex B2B requirements that exceed Shopify Plus B2B capabilities, multi-region setups with strict data residency rules, or businesses with deep existing Magento expertise in-house. For most brands, those scenarios don't apply.

The detailed comparison below walks through each dimension and explains where the difference actually matters.

Cost: build, run, scale

Total cost of ownership is the dimension most brands underestimate when comparing Shopify and Magento.

Shopify Plus runs from £2,000-£2,500/month in licence fees as a starting point. Add app subscriptions (typically £200-£800/month for a scaling brand), transaction fees on certain payment methods, and ongoing agency support. A well-run Shopify Plus operation typically costs £5,000-£15,000 monthly in total platform-and-support spend.

Magento Open Source has no licence fee but requires hosting (£500-£3,000/month depending on traffic), ongoing developer maintenance (£3,000-£10,000+/month for any serious store), and infrastructure security work. Adobe Commerce adds £20,000-£100,000+ in annual licensing on top. Total monthly cost typically exceeds Shopify Plus by 1.5-3x for equivalent stores.

The deeper cost problem is unpredictability. Shopify pricing is flat and forecastable: the same month-on-month unless you add an app or change your plan. Magento costs move whenever the platform does: a botched deployment, a traffic spike that outgrows the current hosting tier, a senior developer leaving mid-retainer. Each event triggers unbudgeted spend.

Time to launch

Speed to launch is one of the clearest differences between the platforms.

A custom Shopify Plus build typically launches in 8-14 weeks. A migration from another platform to Shopify Plus typically runs 8-16 weeks. The reasons are structural: Shopify's hosted architecture removes infrastructure decisions, the platform's opinionated structure reduces architectural debate, and the maturity of the app ecosystem means most integrations are off-the-shelf rather than custom-built.

Magento builds typically run 16-28 weeks because the complexity compounds at every stage. Infrastructure choices add weeks before a line of code is written. Functionality that Shopify covers with an app requires custom development on Magento. Security and performance QA runs significantly longer when the hosting layer is yours to manage. For brands with a fixed deadline (Black Friday-driven, fundraising-driven, or competitive-pressure-driven), Shopify's faster timeline is often the deciding factor regardless of feature comparison.

When Magento makes more sense

  • Complex B2B with multi-tier pricing, contract negotiation workflows, and approval chains that exceed Shopify Plus B2B capabilities
  • Multi-region setups with strict data residency requirements (for example, EU customer data that can't leave the EU)
  • Businesses with deep existing Magento expertise in-house, where the switching cost of retraining a team may outweigh the platform benefits
  • Highly bespoke checkout requirements that exceed even Shopify's Checkout Extensibility (rare, but exists)
  • Enterprise integrations with legacy ERP, OMS, or PIM systems that have Magento connectors but no Shopify equivalent

In our experience, these scenarios apply to fewer than 20% of brands considering the comparison. For the other 80%+, Shopify Plus is the better choice.

When Shopify makes more sense

For the majority of DTC brands and most B2B brands in the sub-enterprise tier, Shopify and Shopify Plus deliver a better operational reality than Magento:

  • Launch timelines are half of Magento in most scenarios: 8-14 weeks for a custom build versus 16-28 weeks.
  • Merchandisers and marketing teams can manage the store without raising a development ticket. On Magento, that's rarely true.
  • Traffic spikes are absorbed at platform level. Shopify has handled every Black Friday since 2014 without requiring store-level infrastructure planning.
  • Most major ecommerce integrations are one-click installs. On Magento, the same integrations typically require scoping, development, and QA cycles.
  • The Shopify developer market is growing. The Magento talent pool is shrinking. Retaining good Shopify developers is easier and cheaper than retaining equivalent Magento expertise.

These advantages build on each other. Developer time saved on maintenance is reinvested in conversion work. Faster iteration cycles mean more tests run, more data gathered, more improvements shipped. The operational gap between the platforms widens the longer a brand runs on Shopify.

Our recommendation

For most scaling DTC brands in 2026, Shopify Plus is the right choice. The total cost of ownership is lower, the operational model is simpler, the time to launch is faster, and the ecosystem is broader.

The exceptions matter. Complex B2B, strict data residency, and deep existing Magento expertise are all legitimate reasons to stay. But they apply to a minority of brands considering the comparison.

Rainy City has been Shopify-only since 2017. That focus is deliberate: we believe Shopify offers the best platform fit for the brands we work with. We don't take a neutral position because we don't have one. Trying to look neutral on a comparison page where one platform is our entire business would be worse than being honest about our perspective.

Key takeaways

  • Magento (Adobe Commerce) still makes sense for complex B2B, strict data residency, or businesses with deep existing Magento expertise in-house.
  • Shopify costs are flat and forecastable. Magento costs are typically 1.5-3x higher when fully loaded, and they move unpredictably when infrastructure events occur.
  • Time to launch on Shopify is roughly half of Magento: 8-14 weeks for a custom build versus 16-28 weeks.
  • Shopify's B2B capabilities have matured significantly since 2023. The historical advantage Magento held in this area has largely closed.
  • Magento developer availability is shrinking while the Shopify talent base grows. Hiring and retaining Shopify specialists is easier and cheaper in 2026.
  • For most scaling DTC brands, Shopify Plus offers better total cost of ownership, faster time to launch, and a broader ecosystem than Magento.
