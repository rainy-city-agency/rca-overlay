Process

The four-step process behind every Rainy City engagement. Tested across 600+ Shopify projects since 2017. You'll always know where things stand.

The four steps

Every project follows the same four-step process: Diagnose, Design, Build, Optimise. Each step has a written output and a sign-off gate before the next step starts. Nothing moves forward without your approval. That is how a 24-week build stays on a 24-week timeline.

Diagnose

Before we design anything, write any code, or commit to any timeline, we audit what’s actually happening: your store, customer behaviour, tech stack, and commercial priorities.

GA4 deep-dive – traffic, conversion, behaviour flow analysis
Heatmaps and session recordings reviewed across key pages
Technical audit - page performance, mobile UX, code quality, third-party app conflicts
Commercial context - your revenue targets, operational constraints, and growth ambitions
Checkout funnel mapped end to end

Design

From the diagnosis, we design with conversion in mind from the first pixel. Every layout decision is tied to a commercial outcome. No decorative choices or design by committee.

What this looks like in practice:

Wireframes for every page template – structure before aesthetics
High-fidelity designs in Figma – branded, mobile-first, conversion-focused
Design system documentation – colours, typography, spacing, components
Stakeholder reviews built into the timeline so there are no surprise final designs
Sign-off gate before development starts – you approve the full design before a line of code is written

Build

Clean, fast, scalable Shopify development. AI-augmented where it adds speed, human-led where it matters. No shortcuts, bloated plugins, or surprise architecture.

Built on Shopify's Online Store 2.0 architecture – your team manages the store without us
Custom sections for every page type – flexibility without developer dependency
Performance optimisation from day one – Lighthouse 90+ at launch
Accessibility audit – WCAG 2.1 AA compliance as standard
Peer-reviewed code via GitHub – no single-developer builds ship without review
Weekly progress reviews – you see what's built, what's next, what's at risk

What you get out of it: A production-ready Shopify store, full handover documentation, and a team that can maintain it independently from day one.

Optimise

Launch is just the beginning. Once the store is live, real customer data starts to flow in. From there, structured A/B testing and ongoing optimisation cycles keep performance moving forward, with progress tracked monthly and reported transparently.

Post-launch monitoring – analytics validation, edge cases, early-traffic optimisation
Structured A/B testing – hypotheses, tests, results, learning
Monthly reporting – what shipped, what we learned, what's next
Test register – every hypothesis tested becomes documented knowledge about your customers
Quarterly strategic reviews – stepping back from execution to revisit direction

What you get out of it: A store that gets more effective with use. Every test cycle adds to a documented understanding of what your customers respond to.

What it's like to work with us

You get one project lead as your direct contact for everything. Behind them is the wider team, but you don’t need to keep track of who does what. Questions go to one person.

Weekly check-ins

Every active project gets a weekly 30-minute call with you and the project team. We cover progress, blockers, next steps, and any decisions required, ensuring you're always up to date.

Single point of contact

Process language is one thing. The actual experience of working with an agency is another. The four steps above explain how the work gets done. Below is what it feels like on your side, day to day.

Transparent communication

If something's going to slip, you hear about it early. If a decision needs your input, we flag it clearly. If we disagree with a direction, we say so. We work with you, not against you.

What we commit to

Fixed timelines

Every project gets a fixed timeline after the Diagnose stage. If we're going to miss it for any reason, you hear about it at the earliest possible moment, with a clear account of what changed.

Fixed costs

The price we quote is the price you pay. If something outside scope comes up, we flag it as a separate piece of work rather than absorbing it quietly into the invoice.

Honest direction

If we disagree with something you're considering, we tell you. If we think the engagement isn't the right next step for your business, we tell you that too. We'd rather lose a project than do bad work.
